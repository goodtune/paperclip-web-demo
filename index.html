How we work

A clear process,
no surprises

Every engagement follows our proven four-phase methodology — built for clarity, quality, and speed.

01

Discovery & Strategy

We start by understanding your business, users, and goals. Stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, and a clear project brief before a single line of code is written.

Deliverables: Project brief · Technical spec · Timeline
02

Design & Prototype

Wireframes first, then high-fidelity designs in Figma. We iterate with you until everything is exactly right before handing off to development.

Deliverables: Wireframes · Design system · Interactive prototype
03

Build & Review

Agile two-week sprints with regular demos. You see real progress every fortnight. Automated testing, code review, and a staging environment throughout.

Deliverables: Sprint demos · Staging site · Test coverage
04

Launch & Grow

Zero-downtime deployment, analytics setup, and a handover session so your team is confident day one. Then we stay on as your ongoing partner.

Deliverables: Live site · Analytics · Training · Retainer
